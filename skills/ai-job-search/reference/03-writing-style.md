# Writing Style Guide

## Critical Rules

1. **NO em-dashes (--).** Use commas, periods, or restructure.
2. **NO cliches.** Cut: "passionate about", "great fit", "leverage skills", "hit the ground running".
3. **NO generic buzzwords** without concrete backing.
4. **NO apologetic language.** Not "I think I could contribute" but "I bring X, demonstrated by Y."
5. **Reframe emphasis, not substance.** Apply the interview backtrack test.

## Tone
- Warm but direct
- Conversational professional
- First person, active voice
- Demonstrate, don't state

## Cover Letter Structure

### Opening Paragraph
- State the role and why (1 sentence)
- Connect background to role (1-2 sentences)

### Body Paragraphs
- Lead with most relevant experience
- Frame around tasks you can solve
- Use bullet lists for concrete skills (3-5 bullets)

### Motivation Section
- Why this specific company
- Reference company mission/values

### Closing
- Brief, confident, forward-looking
