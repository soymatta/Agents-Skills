# Interview Preparation Guide

## STAR Format

**S**ituation → **T**ask → **A**ction → **R**esult

Keep answers to 1-2 minutes. Be specific. End with what you learned.

## Common Questions

### "Why did you leave?"
Be honest, forward-looking, no negativity.

### "You don't have [skill]."
Acknowledge gap, bridge to adjacent experience, show willingness to learn.

### "Why this company?"
Reference specific projects, values, market position. Never generic.

### "Biggest weakness?"
Genuine weakness with concrete mitigation.

## Questions to Ask

### About the Role
- "What does a typical week look like?"
- "What would success look like in 6 months?"
- "What's the biggest challenge right now?"

### About the Team
- "How do you divide work?"
- "What does the lifecycle look like?"
- "How do you onboard?"

### About Culture
- "How would you describe the team culture?"
- "What does professional development look like?"
- "Balance between new projects and maintenance?"
